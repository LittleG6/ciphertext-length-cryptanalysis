## Nonfiction Corpora

The completed nonfiction experiment uses Douglass, Darwin, Thoreau, and Franklin. Its 1,500 archived trial records are stored in `results/reviewer_supplement_20260903/nonfiction_controlled_hc.csv`.

The four modern nonfiction works are included under `corpus/modern_nonfiction_20260926/` as prepared input materials. Their formal search experiments have not been run. Their preparation records are not evidence of completed cryptanalysis experiments on those works.

## MATLAB Scripts

- `scripts/plot_paper_figures_embedded.m` is the original embedded-data plotting script retained from the plotting-code directory.
- `scripts/plot_paper_figures_revised.m` is the separate revised script retained from the experiment-code directory. It includes numerical ciphertext-length axes, supplementary-experiment data, and the revised combined figures.

The two scripts were separate, differently sized files in the original archive despite sharing the same filename. Both versions are included here. They plot embedded values; they do not rerun the cryptanalysis experiments.
